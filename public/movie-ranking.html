The Best Movies according to Varshini

My top 3 movies of all time.

1. Tu Hai Mera Sunday

A light hearted movie about the small things that matter in life.

2. Piku

A film reflecting the life of an independent, self-sufficient, unmarried Indian woman and the complex relationship with her father, who can't take a shit.

3. Lipstick Under My Burkha

Follows the lives of several Indian women across all ages and classes, and how they lead lives while tackling regressive cultural norms.
